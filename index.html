AI observability is becoming part of the assurance conversation

IEEE P4213 is developing a framework for AI-system observability, including the measurements and operational visibility needed to assess reliability and safety. For Physical AI, that pushes assurance beyond model accuracy toward evidence about how the deployed system behaves.
